Controller and scope

Centro Odontológico Aquazul, RNC 132075307. Privacy contact: Juliana Rodríguez, aquazulcod@gmail.com. This information applies to aquazulcod.com and the version published on aquazul.prontialatam.com.

Your choice before analytics

Google Analytics 4 loads only after you accept the Analytics category. Before you decide, after rejection, or when preferences are closed without acceptance, the Google tag is not requested and no analytics signals are sent. We do not use advertising or ad personalisation.

Accept analytics and Reject analytics are offered with equal prominence. Settings lets you save analytics as off or on. Browsing, clicking outside the notice and closing preferences are not interpreted as consent.

Inventory and duration

Necessary: aquazul_privacy, in this domain’s local storage. It saves only the policy version, analytics choice, date and expiry. The choice is valid for six months; validity is checked when you return and a new decision is requested if it has expired or the version has changed. You can also remove it in your browser. It contains no name, email, IP address or patient identifier.

Optional analytics: _ga and _ga_02RW7BEPF7, from Google Analytics. They distinguish browsers and sessions to produce statistics. They are configured with a maximum lifetime of 180 days, without automatic renewal. They are created only after analytics is accepted and are deleted on withdrawal when accessible from this domain.

The Google Analytics property is configured to retain user and event data for two months, without resetting retention on new activity. This period does not limit most of Google’s aggregated statistical reports and is separate from the lifetime of cookies and your privacy choice.

Page, image and font delivery through GitHub Pages uses HTTPS and the browser’s HTTP cache. GitHub states that it logs IP addresses for security. This cache and the hosting logs are distinct from optional analytics cookies.

Measured data and limits

Analytics measures visits and general contact actions only with permission. It sends a generic URL for each language and the name Aquazul; specialty paths, parameters, fragments, messages, enquiry reasons and clinical documents are excluded. A WhatsApp, phone or email click is not a confirmed appointment.

Enhanced measurement and Google Signals are disabled. Advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalisation permissions remain denied. Google Ads, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel and Hotjar have not been added.

Search Console provides aggregate Google search performance and indexing reports. Ownership verification and the sitemap do not insert visitor tracking or create cookies on this website.

Changing or withdrawing permission

You can open Cookie settings in the footer of any page to change your choice. On withdrawal, new events are blocked, Analytics cookies this website can manage are deleted, and the page reloads to remove Google code that has already run. Your rejection is saved so you are not asked again while it remains valid.

Withdrawal does not erase data already received by Google or cookies and sessions you maintain in other services. For requests about data managed by Aquazul, write to aquazulcod@gmail.com. If your browser blocks storage, you may need to choose again when navigating.

External channels and health information

WhatsApp, Google Maps, Instagram and other linked sites open when you follow their links and apply their own terms. Aqua Imágenes displays local content and prepares WhatsApp enquiries; it does not let you upload X-rays or reports to the website. The WhatsApp AI agent is in development and is not yet active.
