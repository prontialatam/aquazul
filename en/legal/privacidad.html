Browsing this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Delivering pages requires processing connection data, such as the IP address and technical request details. You can consult the provider’s privacy information using the link at the end of this page.

The reviewed implementation does not include advertising analytics, tracking pixels or a chatbot. Checks on 2 November 2026 observed no cookies or local storage on the Spanish and English home pages and orthodontics pages. Those checks do not cover external services after following a link.

Enquiries through WhatsApp and email

The appointment selector prepares a WhatsApp link with your selected enquiry reason. You can review the message before sending it. This website does not save that selection in its own database or in the browser’s local storage.

When you contact the clinic through WhatsApp or email, Aquazul receives your contact details and the content you choose to send in order to respond and coordinate your care. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to advertising.

Share only the information needed for your initial enquiry. Ask the team which channel to use for documents or sensitive clinical information.

Appointments, recipients and external services

Aquazul uses an external appointment scheduler. Handling a request may involve the staff attending to it and messaging, email or scheduling providers, depending on the channel used. You can ask Juliana Rodríguez for information about the scheduling provider and the processing applicable to your appointment.

WhatsApp, Gmail email and GitHub are services provided by international providers. Their use may involve processing outside the Dominican Republic under their terms. Information about the data flow and safeguards applicable to a specific enquiry can be requested from the privacy contact.

Google Maps and Instagram are offered as external links. The reviews carousel displays excerpts identified with their source and consultation date; it does not currently import new reviews through an automatic Google connection.

Retention and deletion

The website selector does not create a persistent record of the enquiry reason. To learn about retention of WhatsApp messages, emails and scheduling records handled by the clinic, or to request their deletion, write to the privacy contact. Deletion may be subject to retention obligations applicable to the case.

Your rights

You can request information about your data and exercise applicable rights of access, updating, rectification, objection or deletion under Dominican law. Address your request to Juliana Rodríguez at aquazulcod@gmail.com, explaining what you need. The team may ask for proportionate information needed to verify your identity.

Further information

This page provides information about browsing, initial enquiries and appointment coordination. Consent for dental procedures and information concerning clinical records are handled with the team during care.
