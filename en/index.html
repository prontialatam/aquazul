YOUR ORAL HEALTH COMES FIRST

Your care, step by step.

Dental tourism means arranging dental care away from where you live. At Aquazul, the starting point is an assessment of your case and care coordinated with a well-planned stay.

  1. 01

    Before you travel

    Let’s talk about your smile

    Tell us what you need and request an assessment. Our team will explain which clinical information or previous studies should be reviewed.

  2. 02

    A clear plan

    Your care determines the schedule

    Oral rehabilitation, implants and cosmetic dentistry may require several appointments or stages. Agree on the plan, estimate, recovery time and any further visits before finalising your trip.

  3. 03

    During and afterwards

    Continuity of care

    Agree on your reviews and return-home instructions. Activities, excursions and your return date should fit your treatment and your dentist’s recommendations.

Resources for planning: ADTS health tourism guide · Dominican Dental Association.

A DESTINATION TO EXPLORE AT YOUR OWN PACE

La Romana, between culture and the sea.

On the Dominican Republic’s southeastern coast, La Romana combines culture, beaches and golf. Plan your leisure time around the care you need.

Altos de Chavón

Art, architecture and views of the Chavón River. Explore its streets, galleries and amphitheatre, subject to the venue’s access conditions.

Sea and nature

The region connects with Catalina Island, Bayahibe and Dominicus beaches. Check available excursions and choose activities compatible with your recovery.

Local flavours and life

Discover Dominican cuisine, the marina area and La Romana’s atmosphere. Allow time to rest between appointments.

CURRENCY AND PAYMENTS

RD$ for everyday spending.

The Dominican peso (DOP or RD$) is the official currency. Most local prices are quoted in pesos. You can exchange currency at banks and authorised exchange offices.

US$

Dollars in tourist areas.

Many hotels and tourism businesses accept US dollars. Confirm the quoted currency, exchange rate and currency of any change before paying. Card acceptance depends on the business.
