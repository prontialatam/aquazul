Cookies and technologies

Updated 12 November 2026

Controller and scope

Centro Odontológico Aquazul, RNC 132075307. Privacy contact: Juliana Rodríguez, aquazulcod@gmail.com. This information covers the pages on aquazulcod.com and the version published on aquazul.prontialatam.com.

Review findings

The checks on 12 November 2026 found no first-party or third-party cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage, browser databases, application caches or service workers. No Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel or Hotjar tags were identified.

The review covered the published Spanish and English pages and interaction with the appointment selector, Aqua Imágenes and Dental Tourism. No automatic requests to third-party domains were detected. Images, fonts and scripts are served from the website’s hosting.

Necessary technologies

GitHub Pages delivers pages and their resources over HTTPS. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Connection data is distinct from advertising cookies.

The browser may cache copies of files to speed up later visits, depending on the hosting headers and its settings. The reviewed implementation does not use this technical HTTP cache to create advertising profiles.

Selectors, slideshows, enlarged photographs, and language or temperature controls work with the page content. The website does not save these choices in cookies or persistent storage. The language is identified by the page address.

Analytics, advertising and optional features

The reviewed version has no analytics or advertising tools, or embedded maps, videos, social networks or chats requiring optional consent. It therefore does not display an accept/reject banner or a panel for categories that do not exist.

The absence of a banner does not exempt the contact channels or hosting from privacy obligations. If optional technologies are introduced, this information and the appropriate controls must be reviewed before those technologies are enabled.

External links and WhatsApp

WhatsApp, Google Maps, Instagram and other linked websites open when you choose to follow a link. The external service’s terms and technologies apply from that point. This website does not control its cookies, your signed-in sessions or the retention of its records.

The appointment selector only prepares a message with the selected reason; it does not send an enquiry itself. You can review the message in WhatsApp before sending it. Aquazul’s AI agent is in development and is not yet active.

Available controls

You can manage or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. Data stored in external services must also be managed in those services. For requests concerning messages or appointments you have already sent to Aquazul, contact Juliana Rodríguez at aquazulcod@gmail.com.
