ANSWERS TO YOUR QUESTIONS

Before your
first visit.

Have another question? Let's talk on WhatsApp.

How can I book an appointment?

You can message us on WhatsApp or call +1 809-770-7289. Our team will confirm the available date and time with you.

Where is the clinic located?

You'll find us at Calle Dr. Gonzalvo #33, Downtown Plaza, Suite 103, La Romana, Dominican Republic.

What should I bring to my first consultation?

When arranging your appointment, ask our team which documents or previous studies you should bring for your visit.

Can I ask about costs and payment methods?

Yes. Message us to learn about payment options and your assessment. A treatment estimate depends on the plan recommended for you.

Can I request imaging studies at Aqua Imágenes?

Yes, you can ask about availability using the same contact number. Aqua Imágenes is an independent radiology center located next to Aquazul.
